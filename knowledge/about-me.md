# About Dana

**Source: written by Dana, 2025.**

Dana Reyes is a backend engineer in Portland, Oregon. She graduated from Portland
State University in June 2025 with a B.S. in Computer Science, having already spent
two years at Cascade Freight building the data platform her team's product depended
on.

## How she works

The thing she is actually good at is the seam between a service and its data. Most
of the work at Cascade Freight was not writing new features, it was making existing
ones survive contact with 40 carrier integrations that each disagreed about what a
timestamp means. That meant idempotency, backfills, and retry logic, which is the
part of a system nobody volunteers to own and everybody depends on.

She has a strong preference for making a failure safe rather than making it rare.
The ingestion rebuild is the clearest example: the win was not only that the import
got ten times faster, it was that a partial failure stopped requiring a human to
clean up before the next run could start.

She writes things down. The job scheduler's README names the failure modes it does
*not* cover, because an unwritten limitation is one somebody discovers during an
incident.

## What she is like on a team

Direct in code review and unbothered by being wrong in one. She would rather be
corrected early than ship something she half believes in. She is the person who
asks what happens when this runs twice.

## Outside work

Long distance running, a slow and ongoing effort to learn woodworking, and cooking
that is more enthusiastic than skilled.
