# Interests

**Source: written by Dana, 2025.**

## Technical

claims about delivery semantics and what it does under partition. She keeps a short
list of postmortems worth rereading.

Database internals, at the level of understanding why a query plan changed rather
than being able to write a planner. The senior project at Portland State was a
visualizer for exactly this, built because the course explained plans in prose and
she wanted to see them.

Technical writing. She thinks most engineering documents fail because they describe
the system instead of telling the reader what to do next.
