# Roles Dana is open to

**Source: written by Dana, 2025. This file is injected on every question, so keep it short.**

## What she is looking for

Backend engineering or data platform work, at a company where the data is load
bearing rather than decorative. Logistics, infrastructure, developer tools, and
fintech all fit. She is most interested in teams that measure what they ship.

## Level

Mid-level backend engineer. Two years of production experience plus a CS degree.
Not looking for a staff or lead title yet, and says so plainly rather than letting
a recruiter discover it.

## Location and arrangement

Based in Portland, Oregon. Open to remote within US time zones, hybrid in Portland,
or relocation to Seattle or the Bay Area for the right team.

## Stack preferences

Strong in Python and PostgreSQL, productive in Go. Comfortable being handed a
codebase in something else. Not looking for frontend-heavy work; she can read and
fix a React component but it is not what she wants to do all day.

## Not a fit

Roles that are primarily on-call rotation with no build time, and jobs where the
data platform is one person with no mandate.
